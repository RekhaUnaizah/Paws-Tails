Balanced Pet Diet

Avoid feeding table scraps. Ensure dry kibble is paired with adequate water and essential omega fatty acid supplements.

Coat & Skin Hygiene

Grooming prevents painful mats and skin infections. Brush long-haired pets daily and trim claws every 3-4 weeks.
